What it does

A desk, not a dashboard.

Kagoj does a few things carefully. It works offline, syncs between your iPad, laptop and phone, and keeps a history of every page.

i.Pages & blocks

Headings, toggles, callouts, columns, tables, code, maths (KaTeX), diagrams (Mermaid), highlights in six colours. Type / for anything.

ii.Databases

Table, board, list, gallery, calendar, timeline and chart views. Formulas, templates, repeating rows, side peek.

iii.Calendar & reminders

Classes repeat every Thursday and Saturday. Tasks with dates show up on the calendar, on Home and as reminders.

iv.Handwriting

GoodNotes-style notebooks for Apple Pencil: pen, highlighter, eraser, lasso. Your palm is ignored and fingers scroll. Swipe to turn the page. Handwriting converts to text.

v.Home that talks back

A different greeting each time. It tells you what's next ("INB372 at 11:20"), asks for attendance after class, and lets you jot a task.

vi.Sections

Personal, University, Work. Each course gets attendance, marks, notes and a task list, all wired together.

vii.A work drawer

Projects with quick tasks, plus a shelf for prompts, skills, markdown files and links you keep reusing.

viii.Offline & synced

Everything is saved on the device first, then synced. If two devices edit the same page, both edits are merged block by block.

ix.Yours to take

Version history for every page. Export to Markdown or CSV and import them back. No lock-in.
